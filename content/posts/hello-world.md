I've been building software for a while now, but most of what I learn ends up scattered across commit messages, notes and late-night conversations. This blog is where I want to put it instead.

## What to expect

I'll mostly write about two worlds I spend my time in:

- **Native macOS development** — Swift, SwiftUI and the low-level APIs that make an app feel instant.
- **Full-stack engineering** — Next.js, TypeScript and Java / Spring Boot at scale.

Expect deep dives, small lessons learned and the occasional post-mortem.

> The best way to learn something is to explain it to someone else.
